01 · Architecture & interiors

From concept to render — no CAD, no weeks of work

The whole project lives in one place: plan → interior → render. Hopper reads walls, doors and windows from a photo of a floor plan, and you make changes in plain words: “add a window”, “make it brighter”.

Interiors in any style — shots that look like a photo shoot

“Photo”: an apartment from its floor plan, in daylight and in the evening

Hopper furnishes the plan and shoots the apartment in Blender on our own GPU server. Lighting changes with one click: day or evening, warm 2700 K or cool 6500 K.

02 · 3D

3D models and characters — from an image or a description

Hopper creates textured 3D models for brands, games, marketplaces and merch — around a million triangles from a single image. Studio shots and animation happen in the same place.

Characters, objects, collectibles

Furniture from a single photo

Upload a photo of a piece and enter its size — 29–89 seconds later a 3D model is in the catalogue: facing forward, exactly to size and split into parts you can recolour.

Source photos are public-domain museum images (CC0, Wikimedia Commons). The 3D models were made in Hopper.

03 · Motion design

An animated video from a description

Write a story — Hopper breaks it into scenes, draws the character and brings every frame to life. The character and style stay consistent across scenes: anime, cartoon, 3D or cinematic.

About

I’m building Hopper — one window for all creative work

I’m Marua Makpyr. Hopper is my platform: an architecture studio, a 3D studio and a motion designer working in one browser, without a stack of separate tools.

I lead the product end to end — from the idea and the choice of AI models to how the result looks in the client’s hands. The models run on our own servers, and I only choose ones licensed for commercial use worldwide.
